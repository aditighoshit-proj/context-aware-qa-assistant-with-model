# GEOGO PVT LTD Remote Work and Expenses Policy

## Hybrid Work
GEOGO PVT LTD follows a hybrid model. Core office days are Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Employees may work remotely on Mondays and Fridays.
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time.
Fully remote arrangements require director approval and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Working From Abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. The request needs HR and Security approval and must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

## Home Office Allowance
After probation, employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk and chair.
Internet costs are reimbursed up to $40 per month.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
Business class is not permitted unless a VP approves it in advance.
Hotel stays are capped at $180 per night in standard cities and $250 per night in high-cost cities (New York, London, San Francisco and Singapore).
The meal per diem while travelling is $60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense through the ExpenseHub tool. Receipts are required for every expense over $25.
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days.

## Learning Budget
Each employee has a learning budget of $1,000 per year for courses and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.
Manager approval is required for any single purchase above $500.
