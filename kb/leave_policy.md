# Acme Corp Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month.
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused annual leave days into the next year. Carried-forward days expire on 31 March of the next year.
Unused days beyond the 10-day limit are forfeited and are not paid out in cash. The only exception is resignation or termination, when accrued unused leave is paid out at the basic daily rate.

## Leave Requests
Leave of up to 5 consecutive days needs at least 3 working days' notice. Leave of more than 5 consecutive days needs at least 14 days' notice.
All requests are submitted and approved by the line manager through the PeopleHub portal.

## Probation
Employees in their first 3 months (probation) accrue annual leave as normal, but may take at most 5 days of leave during probation.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward.
A medical certificate is required for any absence of more than 2 consecutive days.
Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of sickness.

## Parental Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 4 weeks of fully paid leave and must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
Adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

## Public Holidays
Acme Corp observes 10 public holidays per year. HR publishes the list each December.
An employee who works on a public holiday earns a compensatory day off, which must be taken within 60 days.

## Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least 1 year of service may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per calendar year. VP approval is required.
