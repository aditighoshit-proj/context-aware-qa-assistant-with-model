# Acme Corp IT Security and Code of Conduct

## Passwords and Access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems.
The company provides a 1Password licence to every employee. Storing passwords in web browsers is prohibited.

## Devices
All company laptops use full-disk encryption. A lost or stolen device must be reported to security@acme.example within 2 hours.
Personal devices may access company email only through the managed mobile app. Company source code must never be stored on personal devices.

## Data Classification
There are four data classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer data is Restricted.
Restricted data must not be shared through personal email or consumer file-sharing services.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected phishing emails must be reported with the Report Phish button in the email client.
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Gifts and Conflicts of Interest
Employees may not accept gifts worth more than $50 from vendors or suppliers. All gifts must be declared to Compliance.
Outside employment requires written approval from HR.

## Harassment and Ethics
Harassment or discrimination can be reported to HR or anonymously through the Ethics Line at ethics.acme.example. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

## Resignation
The notice period is 1 month during probation and 2 months after confirmation.
